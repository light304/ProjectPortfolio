Outlined By The Grid

This box's bounding edges are read straight from the DOM in JS. Any hexagon whose center falls near this rectangle's boundary lights up — so it looks like the hexagons pop up to form the border, even though the box itself has no CSS border at all.

Outlined AND Filled

This one uses data-hex-fill instead of data-hex-highlight — same edge detection, but the hexagons underneath the entire box also light up, not just the border band.

Section Three

Pointy-top hexagons were chosen deliberately — their vertical elongation reinforces the downward scroll direction of a single long-form page rather than a horizontal, multi-panel feel.

Section Four

Keep scrolling — the canvas height matches the full page height, not just the viewport, so the tessellation runs the entire length of the site.
